Platform Choice

The Right Tech for the Right Job

We don't force you into a single platform. We choose the one that fits your catalog, team, and goals.

Best for: Rapid Scale & Zero DevOps

Shopify provides a bulletproof hosted infrastructure. It's ideal for brands that want to focus purely on marketing and merchandising rather than server maintenance.

  • Zero server maintenance or security patching
  • Unbeatable checkout conversion rates
  • Seamless integration with multi-channel POS
  • Shopify Plus available for high-volume enterprise

Best for: Complete Ownership & Custom Data

WooCommerce turns WordPress into a powerful store. It's ideal for brands with complex product data, B2B portals, or those who want to avoid monthly platform transaction fees.

  • 100% data ownership and self-hosted control
  • No mandated transaction fees or platform tax
  • Limitless custom product configurators
  • Deep integration with content-heavy SEO architectures

How We Approach It

The Build Process

No project templates. No guesswork. Every store follows a structured process designed around your specific products, customers, and growth targets.

Phase 01
Discovery & Platform Scoping
We analyse your product range, order volume, target markets, and existing tech stack. We define the right platform, plugin architecture, and integration map before a single line of code is written.
Phase 02
UX Architecture & Wireframing
We map every user flow — product discovery, add-to-cart, checkout, account, and post-purchase. Wireframes are validated against conversion principles before design begins.
Phase 03
Custom Development & Integration
Native Liquid or PHP — no bloated page builders. Payment gateway wiring, third-party API integrations, and custom feature development. We build what you need, not what a plugin approximates.
Phase 04
Performance & Pre-Launch Audit
Core Web Vitals testing, cross-device QA, checkout flow stress testing, and SEO foundation audit. We do not launch a store with a PageSpeed score under 85.
Phase 05
Launch & Handoff
Staged rollout, DNS migration, and post-launch monitoring. Full source code, admin access documentation, and a walkthrough of every custom component. You operate the store independently.
Phase 06
Growth Retainer (Optional)
For brands ready to scale: A/B test builds, new feature sprints, conversion rate optimisation reviews, and platform updates. Fixed monthly engineering time — not hourly billing.
