WooCommerce or Shopify?

Both are solid choices for selling online — the right one depends on how much control you want versus how little you want to manage. Here's the honest breakdown.

WooCommerce
Self-Hosted, WordPress
VS
Shopify
Fully Hosted
You manage your own hosting — more control, but more responsibility.
Hosting
Fully managed for you — nothing to configure or maintain.
No platform fee — you pay for hosting and any premium plugins you choose.
Cost Structure
Monthly subscription, plus transaction fees unless you use Shopify Payments.
Extremely flexible — the entire WordPress plugin ecosystem is available to you.
Customization
Customizable within Shopify's app and theme ecosystem.
More setup involved, and you're responsible for updates and security.
Ease of Setup
Faster to launch, built for people who don't want to touch the technical side.
Scales well with the right hosting — content and commerce can live on one site.
Best For
A dedicated online store you want live fast with minimal upkeep.

Not sure which fits your business? Tell us what you're selling and how hands-on you want to be, and we'll recommend the right one — no pressure toward whichever is easier for us to build.
