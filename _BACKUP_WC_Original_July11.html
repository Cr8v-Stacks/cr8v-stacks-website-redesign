How We Approach It

Full Ecommerce, Full Control

WooCommerce gives you complete control over your store — no platform fee, no app marketplace tax, no ceiling on how complex your product logic can get. The catch is that it requires real engineering to perform well.

We build WooCommerce stores on hand-coded WordPress themes — no Elementor, no pre-built theme to fight against. Payment gateways (Paystack, Stripe, Flutterwave), shipping zone logic, and variable product configurations are wired directly into the build.

Straight Answers

Before You Ask

The questions we get asked most, answered without the corporate hedging.

Do I need WooCommerce or Shopify?

Shopify is simpler to manage and faster to launch — it handles hosting, security, and updates. WooCommerce gives you full code access, no platform fee, and no ceiling on customisation. We'll ask about your product complexity, team, and growth plans and tell you which makes more sense.

Can you wire Paystack for African markets?

Yes. Paystack is our default recommendation for Nigeria-based stores. We wire the gateway directly without a third-party plugin so there's no extra per-transaction fee layered on top.

Do you handle subscriptions and recurring payments?

Yes — we use WooCommerce Subscriptions to handle recurring billing, renewal emails, and subscriber management. Gateway compatibility is confirmed before we start.
