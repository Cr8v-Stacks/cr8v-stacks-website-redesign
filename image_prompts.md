# CR8V Stacks — Overhauled Dark Abstract Image Prompts Guide
*Last updated: 2026-08-13*

## 🎨 Master Visual Direction: "Dark Abstract Tactile Brutalism"

All website images follow a single unified aesthetic framework:
1. **Background**: Pitch-black (`#080808`), obsidian graphite, and dark charcoal canvases ONLY. Absolutely no white or light backgrounds.
2. **Texture**: Fine spraypaint dust grain, subtle stencil geometry, torn paper edge layers, and tactile canvas tooth.
3. **Colors**: Agency primary **Royal Blue (`#0047E1` / `#3D6BFF`)** as the central light/pigment source, paired with controlled, subtle accents of warm gold, crimson red, or warm ivory.
4. **Exclusions**:
   - NO Hero Section images.
   - NO `.webm` / `.mp4` video replacements.
   - NO Case Study or Portfolio showcase images.
   - NO AI Slop (no tech nodes, no wireframes, no sci-fi floating cubes, no grid lines).
