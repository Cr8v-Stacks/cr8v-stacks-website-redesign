## 🎨 Master Visual Framework: "Cinematic Hybrid-Realistic Editorial"

All custom imagery across the website follows a single unified, layout-aware editorial framework.

### Core Architectural Rules
1. **Hybrid-Realistic Human Subjects**:
   - Semi-realistic painterly digital rendering with authentic human facial proportions and natural skin lighting.
   - **Exclusions**: NO flat 2D cartoons, NO exaggerated anime faces, NO generic stock photos, NO AI tech slop (no nodes, HUDs, sci-fi grid lines).
2. **Layout-Aware Negative Space**:
   - Every prompt enforces **rule-of-thirds offset framing** (subject placed on the left or right third).
   - Generates clean, dark obsidian charcoal (`#080808`) negative space on the opposite side to allow UI cards, headlines, or social post text without overlapping the visual subject.
3. **Context-Adaptive Color Spectrum**:
   - **Core Tech / Web Build**: Royal Blue (`#0047E1` / `#3D6BFF`) screen glow & rim light.
   - **Brand Strategy / Scoping**: Warm Amber-Gold & dusk lighting.
   - **SEO / Growth Marketing**: Crimson Sunset & deep warm twilight hues.
   - **Dark Atmosphere**: Deep pitch-black and dark graphite canvases (`#080808`) ONLY.
4. **Dual Aspect Ratio Support**:
   - **Landscape (`16:9`)**: Wide container slots, accordion visual panels, banner graphics.
   - **Portrait / Vertical (`3:4` or `9:16`)**: Split-screen cards, mobile views, vertical showcase slots.

## 🛠️ Universal Prompt Formula Template

```text
[Style Header]: Cinematic hybrid realistic digital editorial illustration, [Aspect Ratio: 16:9 / 3:4]
[Subject & Narrative]: [Specific human subject in an authentic environment, e.g. creator at dusk workspace]
[Framing & Negative Space]: Subject offset to [left/right/lower] third, leaving clean deep dark charcoal (#080808) negative space on [opposite side] for text layout.
[Lighting & Palette]: Dramatic [Royal Blue #0047E1 / Amber Gold / Crimson] key light, dark moody atmospheric shadows.
[Texture & Finish]: Subtle film grain, painterly digital rendering, editorial magazine aesthetic.
[Negative Constraints]: no text, no cartoon, no anime, no white background, no floating tech nodes, no sci-fi HUDs.
```
