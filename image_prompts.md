# CR8V Stacks — Master Image Prompt Engineering Guide
*Last updated: 2026-08-13*

## 🎨 Master System Directives & Guardrails

1. **System-First Visual Rule**:
   - The visual system rules (lighting, color spectrum, negative space, shadow play, rendering texture) dictate the visual identity of every asset.
   - Subjects do **NOT** always have to be human. Image concepts can be literal interpretations of a section, abstract architecture, workspace tools, or conceptual forms, provided they strictly follow one of the 5 Master Visual Systems.
2. **Cultural & Character Representation Directive**:
   - Whenever human subjects ARE depicted, they MUST explicitly feature **mature Black African adults (28–35 age range) with dark skin tones** (e.g. `mature 30-year-old Black African adult male/female founder/developer/strategist with dark skin tone`).
   - Absolutely NO teenage, kid, or flat generic drawing appearances.

---

## 🎨 The 5 Master Visual Systems

### 🟢 System 1: High-Contrast Chiaroscuro Silhouette & Shadow Beams
* **Visual Style**: Minimalist high-contrast graphic editorial poster art. Stylized dark silhouettes or clean architectural forms struck by bold light shafts, casting long sharp shadows across dark obsidian floors.
* **Palette**: Dark obsidian (`#080808`), Royal Blue (`#0047E1`), Sunset Crimson, Warm Amber light shafts.
* **Master Prompt Template**:
  `Minimalist high-contrast graphic editorial illustration, [16:9 landscape / 3:4 portrait] aspect ratio. A stylized dark silhouette of an adult Black African professional walking through a dark minimalist space, struck by a dramatic beam of royal blue (#0047E1) light casting a long sharp shadow across the dark obsidian floor (#080808). High contrast chiaroscuro shadow play, graphic poster art style, deep moody dark atmosphere, clean negative space, no photorealism.`

---

### 🟢 System 2: Mature Adult Lofi + Solid Stylized Silhouette Background
* **Visual Style**: Mature Black African adult characters (28–35 age range, dark skin tone, sleek adult proportions) or central section subjects set against **solid stylized silhouette backgrounds** (simplified dark shapes and monitor/tool outlines) for high visual contrast.
* **Palette**: Dark obsidian charcoal (`#080808`), Royal Blue (`#0047E1`) screen glow, warm amber accent rim light.
* **Master Prompt Template**:
  `Graphic lofi digital editorial illustration of a mature 30-year-old Black African adult tech founder with dark skin tone and glasses, resting head on hand thoughtfully at a desk at night. Soft royal blue (#0047E1) screen light reflecting on his face. Background features solid dark stylized minimalist silhouette shapes (#080808) creating high-contrast graphic contrast. Mature adult facial features, sophisticated cel-shading, moody dark tone, no teenage look, no clutter, no photorealism.`

---

### 🟢 System 3: Rule-of-Thirds Negative Space Layout (Social & Overlay Ready)
* **Visual Style**: Subject (human or conceptual form) offset to the right or left third of the frame, leaving 50–60% clean dark obsidian (`#080808`) negative space for typography, headline text, or UI card overlays.
* **Palette**: Dark obsidian background (`#080808`), contextual brand light accents (Royal Blue, Sunset Crimson, Warm Amber).
* **Master Prompt Template**:
  `Atmospheric graphic digital editorial illustration, [16:9 landscape / 3:4 portrait] aspect ratio. A mature adult Black African female designer with dark skin tone sitting at a minimalist desk, offset to the right third of the frame. The left 60% of the canvas is clean, vast deep obsidian black (#080808) negative space for copy overlay. Soft royal blue (#0047E1) rim light, mature adult features, stylized minimalist silhouette background, moody dark aesthetic, no photorealism.`

---

### 🟢 System 4: Workspace & Abstract Chiaroscuro Fusion
* **Visual Style**: Merges a dark workspace or section subject with an abstract minimalist architectural space where dramatic light beams cut through darkness and cast long sharp shadows across obsidian ground (`#080808`).
* **Palette**: Deep obsidian (`#080808`), Royal Blue (`#0047E1`), dark charcoal shadow gradient.
* **Master Prompt Template**:
  `Atmospheric digital editorial illustration, 16:9 landscape. A mature adult Black African professional sits thoughtfully at a minimalist desk on the right side of the frame, illuminated by soft royal blue (#0047E1) screen light. The dark workspace gradually merges into an abstract minimalist space where a dramatic blue beam cuts through the darkness, creating long sharp shadows across the deep obsidian floor (#080808). Sophisticated cel-shading, graphic silhouette forms, cinematic chiaroscuro, moody lofi atmosphere, clean geometric shapes, strong depth and visual storytelling, vast negative space, premium technology-editorial aesthetic, no photorealism, no clutter, no text.`

---

### 🟢 System 5: Contemporary African Layered Color Patches (Rust, Amber & Deep Red)
* **Visual Style**: Restrained painterly blocks and layered brush-like patches of muted brown, warm rust, burnt orange, and deep muted red integrated into selected dark architectural/silhouette areas, creating uneven multi-tonal depth alongside chiaroscuro shadows.
* **Palette**: Deep obsidian (`#080808`), Royal Blue (`#0047E1`), muted brown, warm rust, burnt orange, deep muted red.
* **Master Prompt Template**:
  `Atmospheric minimalist digital editorial illustration, 16:9 landscape. A mature adult Black African professional sits thoughtfully at a minimalist desk on the right side, illuminated by soft royal blue (#0047E1) screen light and subtle blue rim lighting. Dramatic blue light and long sharp shadows cut through a deep obsidian (#080808) environment, with large areas of intentional negative space. Within some of the dark architectural and silhouette areas, introduce restrained painterly blocks and layered brush-like patches of muted brown, warm rust, burnt orange, and deep muted red, blending two or three tones unevenly as if partially painted onto the scene. Keep these color accents sparse and concentrated in roughly half of selected blank areas, with visible variation between lighter and darker layers rather than smooth gradients. Sophisticated cel-shading, graphic editorial poster style, cinematic chiaroscuro, moody lofi atmosphere, clean geometric forms, strong contrast, premium contemporary African visual aesthetic, no photorealism, no clutter, no text.`
