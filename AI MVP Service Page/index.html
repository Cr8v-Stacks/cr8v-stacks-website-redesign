FAQ

AI MVP & LLM Questions, Answered Honestly

How much does custom web design cost?

Our custom visual web design projects start from $1,200 for high-fidelity interactive Figma wireframes and prototypes. The final price depends on the number of unique page layouts needed, the complexity of interactive elements, and whether custom iconography or brand identity assets are required. We scope every design brief individually and deliver a firm estimate within 24 hours.

How long does it take to design a custom website?

A standard high-fidelity design project—covering wireframes, typography selection, style systems, and interactive Figma mockups for 3-5 pages—typically takes two to four weeks. Enterprise projects with complex interactive dashboards or extensive landing pages take four to six weeks. We provide clear milestone updates and design reviews at every stage.

Do you design in Figma?

Yes. All design work is created inside Figma using organized, responsive auto-layout components. We build clean design style guides containing colors, typography, buttons, grids, and components. When the designs are finalized, you receive full edit and view access to the Figma files, containing organized layers ready for developer handover.

Do I own the design assets and Figma files?

Absolutely. Upon project completion and final sign-off, full ownership of all Figma files, layout structures, custom SVGs, typography sheets, and design system kits is transferred to you. We never lock you into proprietary platforms or hold your brand assets hostage.

Do you write code or build the website after designing?

This service is focused on premium visual UI/UX design and Figma prototyping. However, we are a full-stack studio. If you would like us to build the approved design, we can translate it into a custom Shopify theme, a WordPress custom layout, or a custom PHP/React web application. We can scope the design and development phases together or separately.

How do you design to increase my website conversions?

We design websites for conversion, not just raw aesthetics. Every layout is structured around user profiles, prominent and clear calls-to-action, high-contrast headings, readable typography hierarchies, and intuitive layouts. We map out the exact paths you want users to take to buy, book, or contact, reducing friction at every step.

What deliverables are included in the developer handoff package?

The handover package includes access to the interactive Figma files, responsive page grids, a comprehensive design system UI kit, optimized exportable SVG assets, visual type sheets, and layout specifications. This ensures any developer can translate our designs into code with pixel-perfect accuracy.
