# Agency Digital Ecosystem — Template Breakdown & Architecture

This document tracks the complete breakdown of all standard archive, content, interactive utility, and system error template pages required for a complete digital agency platform.

---

## 1. Blog & Editorial System

- **Blog Main Hub** (`/blog/` or `blog.html`): Filterable grid of all published articles with featured hero story.
- **Single Blog Post Page** (`/blog/single-post.html` or `single-blog.html`): Article layout with table of contents, author bio, read time, inline callouts, and code blocks.
- **Category Archive Page** (`/blog/category/design-systems/` or `blog-archive.html`): Filtered feed showing articles dedicated to a specific core topic.
- **Tag Archive Page** (`/blog/tag/ui-ux/`): Micro-filtered feed based on specific sub-topics or technology tags.
- **Author Profile Archive Page** (`/blog/author/john-doe/`): Bio page displaying all articles authored by a specific team member.

---

## 2. Work & Portfolio System

- **Work / Case Studies Hub** (`/work/` or `Case Studies/index.html`): Visual gallery displaying client success stories and project showcases.
- **Single Case Study Page** (`the-duch-apartments.html`, `blvck-hair-ng.html`, `mkenny-properties.html`): High-conversion project breakdown with metrics, gallery grids, and deliverables.

---

## 3. Toolkits & Interactive Utilities

- **Toolkits Hub** (`/toolkits/`): Directory of agency tools, calculators, and downloadable resources.
- **Interactive Scope & Cost Estimator** (`discovery-call.html` / `/toolkits/website-cost-calculator/`): Interactive scope & cost estimator with dynamic pricing formulas.

---

## 4. System & Utility Templates

- **Search Results Page** (`/search?q=query`): Results layout with search bar, matched articles, services, and zero-results fallback state.
- **404 Error Page** (`404.html`): Animated error page utilizing clean gridline aesthetics and redirecting lost traffic back to core services.
- **Legal & Compliance Pages**: Privacy Policy & Terms of Service.
