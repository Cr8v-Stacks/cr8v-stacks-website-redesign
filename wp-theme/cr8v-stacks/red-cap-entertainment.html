Overview

The Cultural Staging Challenge
& Engineered Solution

Under the creative direction of Farida Atanda, Red Cap Entertainment produces major live cultural experiences, festivals, visual arts exhibitions, and traditional matrimonial showcases spanning London, Sheffield, Lagos, and Accra. Operating across sovereign traditions like the Pan-African Royal Nuptials (Alaga Showcase) and institutional museum exhibitions like Beyond the Bronze, the platform required a high-velocity digital presence that captured the grandeur of live African spectacle.

Rather than wrestling with bloated page-builder JSON that breaks under mobile load, Cr8v Stacks engineered a clean, dependency-free system: converting semantic HTML/CSS directly into a native WordPress Customizer-powered theme, deploying an automated Event CPT docket with real-time past-event calculus, and integrating the universal cr8v-inquiries engine to route high-value production commissions directly into executive briefing dossiers.

01 / Native Customizer Rapid Build Engine

Bypassed Elementor entirely by registering native WP_Customize_Section controls with selective refresh DOM partials and instant postMessage token styling.

02 / Event CPT Loop & Past-Event Calculus

Architected custom date evaluation logic (cr8v_is_event_past()) that automatically disables concluded calendar triggers and tags past productions.

03 / Dynamic .ics Calendar Generator

Engineered zero-plugin downloadable calendar invitations (cr8v_render_calendar_button()) generated in real time from post metadata.

04 / Universal cr8v-inquiries Intake Router

Constructed an anti-spam intake matrix that logs commissioning briefs to admin.php?page=cr8v-inquiries and dispatches branded executive HTML email dossiers.
