Choosing Your Platform

WooCommerce or Shopify?

Both are excellent platforms. The right one depends on what you're optimizing for — pick a priority and we'll highlight what matters most.

CriteriaShopifyWooCommerce
Setup Speed Hosted infrastructure means fewer moving parts to configure before launch.Faster to launch Requires hosting, security, and plugin setup before you're live.
Customization Depth Theme and app-level customization within Shopify's platform rules. Full code access — no ceiling on how deep you can customize.Deepest control
Hosting & Data Control Hosted by Shopify — simple, but your store lives on their infrastructure. Self-hosted on infrastructure you choose and fully own.Full ownership
Ongoing Platform Cost Predictable monthly plan; costs scale with add-on apps. No platform fee — costs come from hosting and plugins instead.
App & Plugin Ecosystem Large, polished app store with consistent quality standards.Most mature Massive plugin library, with more variance in code quality.
Enterprise Scalability Shopify Plus supports high-volume, multi-storefront operations. Scales fully custom — the ceiling is whatever we build to.
Both platforms are genuinely solid choices — the table above highlights trade-offs, not a verdict. We'll help you land on the right one during a positioning audit.
