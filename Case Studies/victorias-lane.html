Victoria's Lane
Shopify Storefront Rebuild

Optimizing a slow, template-based e-commerce store into a high-performance Shopify storefront using basic theme customization, geolocation routing, and catalog structuring.

Client Victoria's Lane
Starting State Slow Theme, High Abandonment
Agency Scope Shopify Dev, SEO & Cataloging
Key Features Geolocation Redirect & Mail Flows

The Challenge

Victoria's Lane is a premium e-commerce retailer specializing in high-ticket luxury handbags and purses. When they approached us, their website was running on a slow, template-based Shopify theme that was not optimized for how users browse products or make purchasing decisions. As a result, they were suffering from a high cart abandonment rate (exceeding 70%) and a slow mobile load time of 4.8 seconds.

The default layouts made it difficult to showcase handbag collections dynamically, and the multi-step checkout introduced significant friction. Since they were relying on direct social media traffic, they needed a fast, high-converting storefront. We were commissioned to customize their layout, optimize page load speeds, and integrate necessary operational tools to drive conversions.

The Solution

We customized a free basic Shopify theme as a base to avoid rebuilding basic e-commerce operations from scratch, while engineering custom layouts and implementing key marketing integrations.

1. Theme Customization & Catalog Layouts

We designed bespoke storefront layouts on top of the free Shopify theme base, creating custom product detail grids, collection views, and navigation menus. We handled the complete product cataloging process, mapping out organized category structures and uploading their premium product library with optimized descriptors.

2. App Integrations & Marketing Operations

We installed and configured essential Shopify apps, including geolocation tags and a multi-store selector to route regional traffic between US and Nigerian storefront environments. We also integrated Klaviyo marketing automation and custom newsletter sign-up pop-ups to capture visitor leads and drive repeat sales.

Our Approach

We executed the Shopify overhaul through a four-phase roadmap, focusing on theme layout customization, catalog configuration, and conversion setup.

01
Deep Audit & Planning

We analyzed handbags buyer shopping behaviors, identifying key drop-off points in the navigation flow and checkout path to guide our optimization efforts.

02
Theme Customization & Styling

Using a free basic Shopify theme as our starting base, we custom-engineered product pages, custom catalog grids, and visual sections to project a premium retail feel.

03
Cataloging & SEO Configuration

We uploaded the handbag catalog, structured search-friendly navigation categories, and carried out detailed search optimization (metadata, URL mapping, alt tagging) to ensure search engine crawlability.

04
Operations & App Integration

We installed regional geolocation redirects, configured multi-store options for US and Nigerian users, and integrated Klaviyo flows and email capture pop-ups.
