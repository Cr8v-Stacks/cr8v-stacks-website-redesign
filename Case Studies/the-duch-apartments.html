Case Study

The Duch Apartments
Brand & Platform from Scratch

Designing a luxury visual identity and custom direct-booking engine for a hospitality brand starting from absolute zero — capturing guest bookings natively.

Client The Duch Apartments
Starting State New Brand, Zero Presence
Agency Scope Identity, Web Design & SEO
Key Features Direct Booking & Local UX

The Challenge

When The Duch Apartments approached us, they were a brand-new business with no digital footprint, no logo, no defined color palette, and no typography systems. They were starting from absolute zero, requiring a complete launch strategy to establish their brand authority.

To avoid massive revenue loss from third-party commission fees, the client wanted to launch with a dedicated direct-booking engine built natively into their own website. We needed to define their visual styling from the ground up and build an organic SEO architecture so they could attract direct guest bookings independently without heavy starting ad budgets.

The Solution

We built The Duch Apartments' digital presence from scratch, delivering a premium visual identity and a custom web platform optimized to host, list, and book rooms natively.

1. Brand Identity & Aesthetic Authority

We designed the brand's logo, established a sleek typography hierarchy, and curated a color palette (utilizing clean white, minimalist space, and a vibrant **emerald green accent** color code `#00BF63`) that projected a modern luxury feel. These visual assets served as the foundation of the project, and are used globally by their team across social channels and marketing campaigns.

2. Direct Booking Engine & SEO Features

We coded an room-listing catalog and direct availability calendar natively on the website, letting guests check suites and complete reservations directly. To drive search traffic and help users navigate, we designed a custom **Closest Attractions section** mapping the apartments' proximity to local hotspots. We paired this with comprehensive web design SEO (metadata optimization, semantic HTML, site speed tuning, and image alt tags) to rank the new domain organically for local queries.

Our Approach

We followed a four-step strategic process to transition the client from a blank canvas to a fully operational, high-converting digital channel.

01
Deep Research & Positioning

Before drafting any visual designs, we spent time analyzing luxury hospitality trends, local hospitality demands, and direct competitor booking flows. This research defined how we positioned the new brand to compete for direct attention.

02
Brand Identity & Colors

We translated our research into visual identity options, testing logos and pairing a fresh green accent color (`#00BF63`) with minimalist white spaces. This styling was refined through interactive feedback loops directly with the client.

03
UX Design & Prototyping

For the portfolio design, we modeled guest checkout pathways. This included wireframing the closest attractions card layouts and availability calendars to ensure room selection was seamless on mobile devices.

04
Bespoke Web Development & SEO

We developed a custom WordPress platform, incorporating semantic markup and custom meta tags. The site's attractions, room descriptions, and speed profiles were engineered concurrently to ensure organic search ranking from launch.
