Bridgepoint Advisory Services
Compliance Analysis Checker

Bespoke document checking portal built by our agency to automate regulatory analysis workflows for an enterprise advisory consulting client.

The Challenge

We were brought in as an agency to handle this project for a client that Bridgepoint Advisory Services was consulting for. Their client processes high volumes of regulatory compliance documents. Their internal analysts and customers were relying on manual spreadsheet processes to check compliance. This manual checking was slow, prone to errors, and difficult to track. They needed an automated application to streamline these checks.

We designed a landing page for the application and built the compliance checker tool. Because the system contains proprietary regulatory rules and sensitive business files, it was slapped behind a secure paywall. When a customer uploads their compliance documents into the application, they must complete a payment to proceed with the audit. All details are described at a high level due to strict client NDA agreements.

The Solution

We custom-developed the complete frontend interface, paywall payment gateways, and backend processing queues for the compliance application, collaborating with a third party who built the automated rules check engine.

1. Multi-Stage Document Auditing & Paywall Integration

We designed the multi-stage document processing workflow. After landing on the design-optimized landing page, the customer uploads their compliance files. The system prompts the user with a payment gateway paywall. Once payment is confirmed, the file passes through several validation stages handled by the automated checking script.

2. Dual Routing & Live Consultation Booking

Upon check completion, the system performs two actions. First, it generates and sends the automated compliance results directly to the user. Second, it presents the user with a dual routing choice: they can either download their compliance reports and exit, or seamlessly book a live consultation session with compliance experts to discuss the results.

Our Approach

We executed the application build through a four-phase development roadmap, balancing secure data schemas with clean dashboard UX.

01
User Flow Design

We mapped out the document upload steps, paywall triggers, and compliance consultation booking flows for their consulting client.

02
Frontend UI/UX Prototyping

We designed the landing pages, payment forms, document staging screens, and post-check consultation calendars.

03
Paywall & Gateway Integration

We integrated secure payment APIs to place the document checks behind a payment gateway, ensuring users must complete payment to proceed.

04
Backend & API Collaboration

We developed backend data routes, collaborating with a third-party partner who built the automated check script to pass files and retrieve compliance results.
