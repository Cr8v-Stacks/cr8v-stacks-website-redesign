Bridgepoint Advisory Services
Brand Identity Launch

Designing the complete visual identity system—logo, typography scales, primary color swatches, and guidelines book—from a blank canvas.

Starting State Blank Page, No Assets
Agency Scope Visual Identity & Logo Design
Key Focus Production-Ready Formats

The Challenge

Bridgepoint Advisory Services came to us seeking to establish a premium corporate presence. They were starting from absolute zero, with no logo, no color definitions, no fonts, and no brand guidelines. To compete in their industry and secure trust from high-level clients, they needed a cohesive visual identity built entirely from scratch.

The visual identity had to project modern corporate authority and scale seamlessly across all channels. We were commissioned to build a custom visual identity system, hand-drawing vector logo structures, defining typography hierarchies, and developing a comprehensive brand guidelines manual to secure design consistency for all future business assets.

The Solution

We designed their complete visual identity, delivering bespoke vectors, typography systems, and print-ready style manuals.

1. Custom Logo System & Vector Marks

We designed their logo mark from a blank canvas, drawing custom vector paths and avoiding all pre-made shapes, stock designs, or AI generators. The resulting mark scales cleanly from tiny mobile icons to massive physical signage files.

2. Typography Pairing & Brand Guidelines Manual

We established a sleek corporate type scale and curated a palette utilizing professional deep navy blue, slate grey, and clean spaces to build trust. We compiled all guidelines (logo usage guidelines, color rules, spacing restrictions) into a master brand manual for their staff.

Our Approach

We executed the brand design through a four-phase roadmap, focusing on custom vector drawing and complete identity guidelines compilation.

01
Identity Scoping

We audited corporate visual trends and competitor style guidelines to define the aesthetic parameters for the Bridgepoint Advisory Services mark.

02
Bespoke Logo Vectoring

We hand-drew custom vector marks, refining paths and line weights through multiple iterations directly with their executive team.

03
Typography & Swatches

We defined font hierarchies, paired headlines and body systems, and structured the brand's primary and secondary color palettes.

04
Brand Guidelines Assembly

We compiled all logo constraints, color rules, and layout templates into a comprehensive guidelines manual ready for immediate distribution.
