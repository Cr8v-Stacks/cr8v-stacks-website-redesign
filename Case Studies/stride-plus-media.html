Brand Strategy & SEO Foundation

The Challenge

Stride Plus Media wanted to launch Stride Radio, a dynamic digital radio streaming and entertainment platform. However, they approached us with no defined brand positioning, no clear target listener profiles, and no organic visibility. To launch successfully, they needed a comprehensive brand strategy that incorporated a solid search engine optimization (SEO) foundation and mapped out their streaming platform launch.

The client needed a dedicated partner who could design the brand strategy and carry that exact positioning and SEO architecture through the design of their landing pages and custom web streaming platform. Rather than splitting design and positioning across multiple agencies, they hired our team to execute the strategy and web platform under one unified voice.

The Solution

We developed their brand strategy, established their search engine optimization (SEO) foundation, designed their website and landing page, and configured their streaming app.

1. Brand Strategy, SEO Foundation & Web Platform

We designed Stride's brand strategy from scratch, defining their target listener demographics, competitive messaging guidelines, and organic SEO structures. We then built their custom web streaming platform and promotional landing pages, ensuring search engines could index their programming easily.

2. Mobile App Collaboration & Configuration

Instead of custom-developing the mobile application code in-house, we collaborated with an external third-party developer to bring the mobile app to life. Once the application was built, we handled the complete configuration of the streaming APIs and backend connections to link the mobile app seamlessly with the web station.

Our Approach

We executed the Stride Radio launch through a four-phase development roadmap, connecting brand strategy with platform design and API setup.

01
Brand & SEO Strategy

We mapped out the brand positioning and targeted keywords to establish the site's organic SEO and categorization architecture.

02
Web & Landing Page Design

We designed the brand's visual identity, marketing landing page, and streaming website layout templates.

03
App Collaboration

We collaborated with a third-party developer to code the mobile streaming app, ensuring visual guidelines were maintained.

04
API & System Integration

We configured the app's streaming endpoints and APIs to link both web and mobile listeners to a single audio feed.
