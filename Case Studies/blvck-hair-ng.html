The Challenge

blvck Hair NG is a premium luxury hair production company. When they initially reached out to us, they already had a functional Shopify e-commerce website. Their previous developers had marked the site as "SEO-optimized," but the brand was experiencing zero organic search traffic. Their search visibility was practically non-existent, leaving them entirely dependent on direct Instagram messaging and high social media ad spend to drive sales.

Upon auditing, we discovered that they had duplicate product URLs, zero structured schema data, no indexable meta tagging, and generic, unoptimized thin copy. Buyers looking for raw human hair extensions online were completely routed to legacy local directories or older competitors. We were brought in to perform a proper, technical search optimization job and build a sustainable organic funnel.

The Solution

We designed and executed a multi-phase SEO strategy focused on search intent mapping, metadata restructuring, and comprehensive product catalog copy optimization.

1. Intent Mapping & Keyword Research

We began by mapping transactional search terms specific to luxury hair buyers, clustering keywords like "raw temple virgin hair" and local search tags for regional customer centers. We built a structured keyword matrix to map intent to specific product catalog endpoints.

2. Product & Category Description Copywriting

We rewrote all product descriptions from scratch to include semantic keywords and structured headings, and wrote custom, search-optimized copy for catalog and category pages. This ensured search engines could index category hubs cleanly for broad search queries.

Our Approach

We executed the search optimization through a four-phase development roadmap, balancing technical crawling fixes with high-relevancy copywriting.

01
Deep Keyword Research

We mapped out high-relevancy search terms for premium human hair extensions, identifying transactional search keywords like "raw virgin hair extensions" and mapping them directly to catalog categories.

02
Technical Crawl Optimization

We audited their Shopify build to consolidate duplicate catalog URLs using clean canonical tagging, structured their page headings, and set up indexable meta tags on all collection templates.

03
Product Catalog Copywriting

We rewrote all thin product descriptions and category-level introductory copy, crafting rich, search-optimized content that described textures, origins, and styling options.

04
Authority Blog Boost

To accelerate domain authority at launch, we authored a series of high-quality, informative blog articles on hair styling and care tips, creating immediate organic search ranking signals.
