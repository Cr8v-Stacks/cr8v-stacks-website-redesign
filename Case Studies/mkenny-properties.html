Overview

The Strategic Challenge
& Engineered Solution

Mkenny Properties was limited by standard real estate themes that were rigid, hard to customize, and slowed down by heavy database queries.

They needed custom property archive filters (location, price range, bedrooms), interactive floorplan viewers, and instant WhatsApp inquiry widgets.

01 / Custom Elementor & PHP Widgets

Engineered bespoke Elementor widgets allowing the client team to build custom property pages effortlessly.

02 / High-Speed Property Query Engine

Optimized WordPress WP_Query database calls for instant filtering across hundreds of active listings.

03 / Interactive Map & Location Integration

Embedded Google Maps API boundaries highlighting neighborhood amenities and transport hubs.

04 / Direct WhatsApp Inquiry Webhooks

Built one-click lead capture buttons connecting interested buyers directly to sales agents.
