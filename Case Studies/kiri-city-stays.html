Kiri City Stays
Digital Marketing & Brand Launch

Designing the visual identity, configuring Google Tag Manager conversion events, and executing high-yield Google Search and Facebook ad campaigns for UK serviced apartments.

Client Kiri City Stays
Starting State No Tracking, Low Sales
Agency Scope Brand Identity & Ad Campaigns
Key Channels Google Tags, Google & FB Ads

The Challenge

Kiri City Stays is a premium serviced apartment operator based in the UK. When they engaged us, they had a basic online presence but lacked any structured digital marketing tracking, conversion metrics, or customer acquisition loops. They wanted to drive direct bookings through search and social ad channels, but were suffering from high acquisition costs and zero attribution data.

They needed a unified partner who could design a polished brand identity (logo, color system, typography), implement a reliable conversion tracking pipeline (Google Tag Manager), and deploy targeted search and social campaigns. The entire acquisition funnel had to run under a single consistent voice to convert high-value business and leisure travelers.

The Solution

We designed their complete visual identity, configured Google Tag Manager event tracking, built a core website alongside multiple ad-conversion landing pages, and deployed connected ad campaigns across Google and Facebook.

1. Visual Branding & Google Tag Tracking Setup

We custom-designed their corporate logo system, established visual guidelines, and defined their typography and colors (navy charcoal `#1E293B` and warm gold `#D97706`). To ensure 100% attribution accuracy, we configured Google Tag Manager and conversion events to track booking button clicks and reservation submissions precisely.

2. Landing Pages Design & Search Ad Campaigns

We designed and built a main website alongside multiple custom landing pages for specific serviced apartment locations to optimize campaign conversions. We launched and managed targeted Google Search ads and Facebook campaigns. By routing ad traffic directly to optimized landing pages and adjusting bids based on real-time conversion tags data, we reduced customer acquisition costs and drove bookings.

Our Approach

We executed the marketing launch through a four-phase development roadmap, connecting brand design with conversion tracking and targeted ads.

01
Funnel Scoping

We audited UK serviced apartment keywords and visitor demographics to identify high-intent target markets for Google Search campaigns.

02
Brand Identity Design

We designed the brand's logo, colors system, and typography layout guidelines to project corporate authority and trust.

03
Conversion Tracking Integration

We implemented Google Tag Manager on the website, defining conversion tags, triggers, and parameters to track booking conversions.

04
Ad Campaigns Execution

We deployed targeted Facebook and Google Ads campaigns, adjusting bids and messaging copy to maximize booking conversions.
