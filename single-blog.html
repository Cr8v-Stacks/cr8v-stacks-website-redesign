Our own site runs on custom zero-plugin architecture. That's not a casual detail — it's the result of a deliberate decision about what a design studio's marketing site needs to be, and it's the reason I have strong opinions about who liquid performance engineering is right for and who it isn't.

Zero-plugin custom development is the best tool I've used for building fast, beautiful, design-forward marketing sites. It's also genuinely not the right home for every single blog on earth. So before I walk through the migration, I want to be straight with you about when this move actually makes sense, because the wrong migration is worse than no migration.

What Liquid Engineering Is Actually For

Custom framework theme engineering started as a prototyping approach and grew into a full site paradigm. The canvas behaves the way designers think, the animation and interaction capabilities are excellent without code bloat, and the output is fast.

Where custom zero-plugin architecture wins:

  • Marketing and brand sites — homepages, product pages, landing pages, campaign sites
  • Startup sites that need to look exceptional and ship quickly
  • SEO Authority platforms — zero layout shift (CLS < 0.01) with sub-50ms INP execution
"Liquid performance engineering is the discipline of stripping away framework bloat, writing native theme code, and delivering 100% Core Web Vitals pass rates on launch day."
