Platform Choice

WooCommerce vs Shopify

Both are world-class platforms — the right one depends on your business model, ownership preferences, and growth roadmap. We work on both, and we will tell you honestly which one fits your situation.

WOO
WooCommerce
Open-Source · WordPress Ecosystem
  • ✔Full ownership — your code, your server, your data with zero platform lock-in
  • ✔Deeply extensible PHP — custom post types, hooks, REST API, bespoke plugins
  • ✔Ideal when you already run WordPress or need complex content + commerce fusion
  • ✔No per-transaction fees — your revenue stays yours on any payment gateway
  • ✔Unlimited product variations, custom pricing logic, and subscription models
  • ✔Lower long-term hosting costs at scale vs. Shopify's escalating plan tiers
Best For
Businesses that want full code control, complex custom logic, existing WordPress infrastructure, or subscription-heavy models.
SHY
Shopify
Hosted SaaS · Liquid Templating
  • ✔Fully managed infrastructure — hosting, SSL, PCI compliance handled by Shopify
  • ✔Liquid templating allows deep front-end customisation without server management
  • ✔Native Shopify Payments, multi-currency, and Markets for global selling
  • ✔Best-in-class mobile checkout — Apple Pay, Google Pay, Shop Pay built-in
  • ✔Metafields, Metaobjects, and Storefront API for headless or custom workflows
  • ✔Fastest time-to-market for product-heavy stores with no DevOps overhead
Best For
Brands launching fast, scaling internationally, or prioritising managed reliability and native payment tooling over code ownership.

How We Approach It

The Build Process

No project templates. No guesswork. Every store follows a structured process designed around your specific products, customers, and growth targets.

Phase 01
Discovery & Platform Scoping
We analyse your product range, order volume, target markets, and existing tech stack. We define the right platform, plugin architecture, and integration map before a single line of code is written.
Phase 02
UX Architecture & Wireframing
We map every user flow — product discovery, add-to-cart, checkout, account, and post-purchase. Wireframes are validated against conversion principles before design begins.
Phase 03
Custom Development & Integration
Native Liquid or PHP — no bloated page builders. Payment gateway wiring, third-party API integrations, and custom feature development. We build what you need, not what a plugin approximates.
Phase 04
Performance & Pre-Launch Audit
Core Web Vitals testing, cross-device QA, checkout flow stress testing, and SEO foundation audit. We do not launch a store with a PageSpeed score under 85.
Phase 05
Launch & Handoff
Staged rollout, DNS migration, and post-launch monitoring. Full source code, admin access documentation, and a walkthrough of every custom component. You operate the store independently.
Phase 06
Growth Retainer (Optional)
For brands ready to scale: A/B test builds, new feature sprints, conversion rate optimisation reviews, and platform updates. Fixed monthly engineering time — not hourly billing.
